# Frequency source-custody audit

Audit date: 2026-10-26. Scope: all twelve `corpus/us-*.md` records.

The repository retains twelve round-trips/day quantities with one broad
`wikipedia-amtrak-routes` reference. No original timetable bytes, exact article
revision, route/date/segment/direction/operating-day locators or independently
retained corroboration substantiate those quantities here. All twelve are now
`source-needed`. Missing custody does not establish that a value is wrong.

Hashes and published copies establish custody of derivative repository records,
not original timetable verification. Native/WASM JSON now carries the original
quantity's actual evidence label, false primary-source verification and explicit
unknown service observations even when every hypothetical score clears its bar.
The browser publishes the audit without requiring WASM.

## Grain and interpretation

The 2023/2024 label is retained historical context, not a per-record observation
window or current timetable. Route/brand names and endpoint labels do not establish
whole-corridor or all-segment service. Per-brand NEC entries are not independent
proof of complete endpoint coverage or usable connections. Do not add unlike
route scopes into a nationwide service-frequency conclusion.

Round trips/day is a frequency proxy. It does not measure first/last departures,
service span, spacing, operating days, trip time or reliability. The retained
Sunset Limited 0.4/day is a rounded claim: it is not exactly 3/7 and does not mean
service operates every day. Its separate historical three-per-week description
also needs a dated timetable. The provisional 7.0 bar and score transform are
model choices. With integer daily inputs, 11 maps to 6.9 and 12 maps to 7.5 under
the existing one-decimal transform; neither clears an actual service promise.

Eight of twelve retained scores fall below that model bar. That establishes the
native detector's behavior on this selected distribution, not a representative
national deficit, a validated span result or an instruction to upgrade routes.
Twelve other dimensions remain unassessed; tier metadata is not SLA conformance.
