What remains unassessed

Trip time, reliability, ridership, modal shift, costs, host-track capacity, access equity and the other DIM-01..13 dimensions remain unassessed here. Adequate frequency alone is not a full service promise, a build recommendation or a national finding.

The historical baseline preserves committed scores and counts from the source-labelled corpus. Every edited corridor changes in your hypothetical scenario. Unedited corridors retain their historical values. The bar applies to both runs. Rust uses GAUGE's existing gap detector: the bottom quartile must fall below the bar to trigger a dispersion signal; at least half of scored corridors below the bar is classified as systemic, otherwise as a concentrated tail. “No frequency dispersion gap” refers only to this dimension.
